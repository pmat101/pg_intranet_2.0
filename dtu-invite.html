Respected Sir,

The EIA Notification 2006 and its subsequent amendments constitute the principal statutory instrument governing Environmental Clearances for industrial and infrastructure projects in India. The period 2025–2026 has witnessed a series of significant legal and administrative developments — including the MoEF&CC Office Memorandum dated 29 January 2025 on EC validity, transfer, and split provisions; progressive amendments to the compliance and monitoring framework; and the rollout of PARIVESH 2.0 — that have materially altered the obligations of project proponents and the working of the regulatory apparatus alike.

There exists, however, a considerable gap between the regulatory intent behind these instruments and their practical understanding at the level of industry, particularly in the MSME and mid-scale manufacturing segments. To bridge this gap through structured, authoritative engagement, Perfact Group is organising the Environmental Conference 2026 on Saturday, 06 June 2026 at the India Habitat Centre, New Delhi, with the Department of Environmental Science & Engineering, Delhi Technological University as Co-organiser.

As DTU is co-organiser of the event, it would be a matter of great honour for the organising committee if you would grace the Inaugural Session and deliver the Welcome Address to mark the commencement of the proceedings. Your presence would send a clear signal of the University's support for the industry's pursuit of sustainable development.

The Additional Secretary or Joint Secretary of MoEF&CC is expected to attend along with other senior officials of the Ministry. The audience is expected to comprise 120–150 senior participants, including EHS and environment heads from industry, project directors, environmental consultants, representatives of State Pollution Control Boards, and members of academic and research institutions. The proceedings of the conference shall be formally compiled and submitted to MoEF&CC for policy consideration.

The formal invitation letter, on the letterhead of Perfact Envirosolutions Pvt Ltd, is attached for your kind reference and record. I would be grateful for the opportunity to call on you personally at a time of your convenience to extend the invitation and to apprise you of the proceedings in greater detail.

Kindly let us know a time that suits your schedule.
